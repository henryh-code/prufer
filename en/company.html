Company

Over 70 years of experience

From the Solingen steel industry to research institutes and customers on every continent: the history of Prüfer Industrieofenbau.

  1. 1935

    Hans Prüfer Industrieofenbau was founded in Düsseldorf on 2 April 1935. In its early years the company built furnace systems exclusively for the Solingen steel industry. Around 600 furnace systems for tempering, annealing and hardening steel were delivered there until 1960.

  2. 1953

    First export of a furnace system, to a steel-processing company in Finland.

  3. 1960

    In close cooperation with Ruhrkohle AG, furnaces for determining quality, environmental compatibility and coking technology were developed, refined and optimised, and later adapted to DIN, ISO and ASTM standards. These instruments are in use worldwide and represent state-of-the-art technology. The former mining research institute, today DMT in Essen, remains an important partner to this day.

  4. 1964

    The focus shifted to quality control at steelworks such as Thyssen, Hoesch, Deutsche Edelstahlwerke, Krupp and Mannesmann. Together with the research institutes of these steelworks, and building on almost 30 years of experience in the Solingen steel industry, furnace systems were developed that – through continuous improvement – are still built today.

  5. 1968

    A fourth pillar of research followed through another client: the Max Planck Institute for Iron Research in Düsseldorf. Afterwards, several departments of the research centres in Jülich and Karlsruhe joined the partnership.

  6. 1971

    Development and manufacture of hot-drawing furnaces and creep furnaces with the corresponding measurement and control technology.

  7. 1975

    The product range is expanded with furnaces for medical technology, with a particular focus on dental technology.

  8. 1980

    The company headquarters moves within Düsseldorf, as the old buildings no longer met the demands of production.

  9. 1987

    ALLINO's laboratory products and small-furnace production in Düsseldorf are integrated into LOI in Essen by mutual agreement, ensuring continued service and spare-parts supply.

  10. 1988

    Dipl.-Ing. Hans Prüfer Industrieofenbau KG becomes Dipl.-Ing. Hans Prüfer Industrieofenbau GmbH. The company is managed by Willi Kipka and Manfred Theisen Sr., who had led it since 1960 and 1964 respectively.

  11. 1990

    Willi Kipka leaves the company; Manfred Theisen Jr. joins the management.

  12. 1996

    Manfred Theisen Sr. leaves the company.

  13. 1997

    Production of the Ruhr dilatometer and the Gieseler plastometer begins; the first instruments go to South Africa. They are a further development of instruments previously distributed by Hüter GmbH in Essen, which ceased production after the death of its owner, Albin Strauch. After a long partnership, Hans Prüfer Industrieofenbau GmbH takes over production.

  14. 1999

    The company headquarters moves to Neuss.

  15. 2000

    Joint development of furnace systems with Rubotherm Präzisionsmesstechnik GmbH in Bochum.

  16. 2002

    With installations in Korea and a system delivered to Shanghai, the gateway to Asia opens. INTEC LTD (Dr. Kim) is secured as partner for the Korean market.

Never stand still

Since the first export in 1953, furnace systems have been delivered to 39 countries. The path paved by Hans Prüfer, Willi Kipka and Manfred Theisen Sr. is continued by today's team.

Their philosophy: never stand still, and keep manufacturing and development at the highest level.
