Furnace construction

From steel to medical technology

What began in 1935 with furnaces for the Solingen steel industry has grown into a broad range of furnace systems for production, research and quality control.

  • Tempering, annealing and hardening furnaces
  • Chamber, shaft, belt and tube furnaces
  • Creep and hot-drawing furnaces with matching measurement and control
  • Laboratory and workshop furnaces
  • Furnaces for medical and dental technology

Testing instruments

Coking technology and coal testing

In close cooperation with Ruhrkohle AG we developed furnaces for determining quality, environmental compatibility and coking technology, adapted to DIN, ISO and ASTM standards. Since 1997 we also build the Ruhr dilatometer and the Gieseler plastometer.

  • In use worldwide
  • Long-standing partnership with DMT in Essen
  • Instruments delivered from South Africa to Korea
